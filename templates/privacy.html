Privacy Policy

CipherShield is an educational security project. In a deployed instance, account details and security events may be stored to provide authentication and security features.

Sensitive profile fields are encrypted before storage. Passwords are stored as password hashes, not plaintext. Do not enter real personal or confidential information into this demonstration application.

Administrators should configure secure secrets, HTTPS, database access controls, backups, and a retention policy before production use.
